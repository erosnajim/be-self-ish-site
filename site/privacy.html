Self-ish — Privacy Policy

The short version: your nights stay yours. We don't sell, share, track, or advertise.

Who this is for

Self-ish is made for adults. The app asks you to confirm you're 18 or older before you begin.

What we collect, and why

Everything below is kept only to make the app work. None of it is used for tracking, advertising, or marketing by anyone.

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in and help you recover your account
Your clips, Owl puzzle videos, and mood selfiesSo your nights can be backed up to the cloud
The sound in those clipsIt's part of the video
Your night summaries and clip notesSo your book remembers them
Lion's move log (which move, when)So Lion can keep track of your quick moves
Your account idTo connect your backups to your account
Your Plus subscription statusVerified with Apple, to know what's unlocked
Crash reportsTo fix bugs (see below)

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, browsing or search activity, advertising data, health records, or any financial details beyond the subscription status Apple shares with us.

Where it's stored

The originals live on your phone first. Cloud backups are kept in private storage on Supabase (servers in Sydney, Australia). Each account can only reach its own files.

How long we keep it

  • Free accounts: video clips are removed from the cloud after 30 days (the originals stay on your phone). Your mood selfies, notes, night summaries and Owl and Lion records stay backed up until you delete your account.
  • Self-ish Plus: backups are kept while you're subscribed. If Plus ends, you get a 15-day grace window to download. Owl puzzle videos that were backed up under Plus are removed 46 days after Plus ends.

Deleting everything

In the app, go to You → Delete my account. This deletes your account, every cloud file, and every record, immediately. Nothing is recoverable afterwards, and it revokes Sign in with Apple. Apple subscriptions are cancelled separately in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Payments

All payments are handled by Apple. The app never sees your card details.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, we receive an error report through Sentry. It carries an account id and error codes only, never your reflections, file names, or messages.

What we never do

No selling. No sharing. No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. Your reflections are never public and never shown to anyone else.

Contact

Questions? Email support@be-self-ish.app.
